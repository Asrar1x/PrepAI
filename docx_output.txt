1. Executive Summary
PrepAI is a full production-grade, end-to-end AI placement interview preparation platform built for Indian engineering college students. It is not a static question bank and not a basic quiz app. PrepAI is a complete system covering every stage from a student's first role exploration to their final interview-readiness report — with a real-time AI voice interviewer at its core.
The 2026 campus placement landscape has fundamentally changed. Only ~10,000 active entry-level tech roles exist nationally (Xpheno, Jun 2026). Infosys targets 20,000+ fresher hires this year. TCS NQT remains the highest-volume assessment. Wipro targets 10,000–12,000 hires. Competition per seat is at its highest since 2020. Students who prepare generically fail. Students who prepare specifically — by role, by company pattern, with simulated interviews — get placed. PrepAI solves this with AI.
2. Problem Statement & Market Context 2026
2.1  The 2026 Campus Placement Reality
Infosys plans 20,000+ fresher hires for 2026, with salary tracks from ₹6.25 LPA (DSE) to ₹21 LPA (SP L3). TCS NQT runs 3 sections: Verbal, Quantitative Aptitude, and Coding. Wipro targets 10,000–12,000 hires via NSET. The Infosys SP L3 track offers ₹21 LPA, L2 offers ₹16 LPA, L1 offers ₹10 LPA, DSE offers ₹6.25 LPA. The difference between landing L3 vs DSE — ₹14.75 LPA — is entirely determined by interview performance quality. Students who cannot clearly articulate knowledge, handle aptitude under time pressure, or manage counter-questions in a live interview end up in the lowest salary band or unplaced.
2.2  The Four Failures of Current Placement Training
Static Content Problem: Training departments give students PDFs of 200 questions written in 2019. Amazon's current format is completely different. TCS NQT 2026 has different section timings and patterns than prior years. The PDF is outdated the day it is printed.
Impact: Students memorise stale content that does not match actual 2026 test patterns
Zero Simulation Problem: Students read about interviews forever but freeze when counter-questioned live. Reading about interviews is not interviewing. There is no pressure, no turn-taking, no composure test.
Impact: Students fail HR rounds not on knowledge but on delivery and psychological composure
No Feedback Loop Problem: After a college mock interview, the trainer says 'good, needs improvement.' That is not data. Students don't know which topics they are weak in, how many filler words they use, or their average response latency.
Impact: Preparation is blind. Students cannot measure or improve what they are not tracking.
No India-Native AI Tool Problem: Final Round AI, Yoodli, and Interview Sidekick are US-focused, charge $20–50/month USD, and have zero knowledge of TCS NQT, Zoho's 5-round structure, or Infosys SP salary tracks.
Impact: No affordable AI interview simulation product exists for Indian college placement preparation in 2026

5.3  Background Job Flow (Post-Interview)
After session ends, the student sees 'Processing your report...' instantly. The heavy work runs async via Celery so no user waits for a synchronous AI call chain.
Interview end signal → FastAPI creates Celery task with session_id → Redis queue
Celery worker: fetch full transcript from PostgreSQL → call Claude Haiku 4.5 with feedback prompt → receive structured feedback JSON
Worker: generate PDF via ReportLab (score radar chart + question table + communication stats) → upload to Cloudflare R2
Worker: update interview_sessions row (overall_score, score_breakdown, feedback_report, report_url, status='completed')
Worker: send Resend email + WhatsApp Business API message with report link
React frontend polls /interviews/{id} every 3 seconds (TanStack Query) → shows report when status changes to 'completed'
6. Module 1 — Role & Company Intelligence Engine
6.1  Purpose
Before touching a single question, students must understand what the role truly demands, how the company structures its interview, and where their personal skill gaps are. This module provides that intelligence — dynamically, not from a static PDF.

6.4  Skill Gap Analyzer
Student selects target role + target company
System renders a skill checklist of 30–50 competencies required for that role
Student marks each: Strong / Okay / Weak / Not Learned
Claude Sonnet 4.6 analyzes the gap matrix and generates a personalized 30-day study plan
Plan broken into daily topics with estimated study time and curated resource links
Study plan saved to study_plans table, progress tracked on dashboard
6.5  Question Pattern Explainer
For every question type in company assessments, Claude explains: what the question actually tests beneath the surface, what a weak answer looks like vs a strong answer, and the interviewer's true scoring criteria. This transforms passive reading into strategic preparation.
7. Module 2 — Dynamic Aptitude Test Engine
7.1  Architecture
Every aptitude session generates fresh questions via Claude Haiku 4.5. No two sessions serve identical questions. Company Mode exactly mirrors the section structure, time limits, and question distribution of the selected company's 2026 online assessment format.
7.2  Question Categories — 7 Types
7.3  Company Pattern Modes — 2026 Exact Formats
7.4  Adaptive Difficulty Engine
Correct streak of 3 consecutive: difficulty index increases by 1 (Easy → Medium → Hard)
Wrong streak of 2 consecutive: difficulty index decreases by 1
Final score adjusted for difficulty level reached — harder correct answers score higher
Difficulty trajectory stored and visualized in session report as a line graph
Separate difficulty trackers per category within the same session
7.5  AI Solution Engine
Every wrong answer triggers Claude Haiku 4.5 to generate a step-by-step solution
Solution includes: concept name, formula if applicable, full working, common mistake explanation, memory tip
Solutions cached by question_id in Redis (1-week TTL) — same question never regenerated
Student upvote/downvote on explanations — flagged for admin review if downvoted 3+ times
7.6  Daily Practice Scheduler
Celery Beat runs daily at 8:00 AM IST for all active students
Generates personalized 20-question set targeting student's weakest sub-topics from past 7 days
Email (Resend) + WhatsApp notification with deep-link to start daily test
Completion earns daily quest XP and maintains streak
