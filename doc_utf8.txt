1. Executive Summary
PrepAI is an AI-powered placement interview preparation platform designed specifically for engineering college students entering their placement season. The platform solves a critical gap in current placement training: students know they need to prepare, but they do not know HOW companies actually ask questions, WHAT interviewers truly evaluate, and HOW to simulate a real interview environment before the actual D-day.
PrepAI delivers three core capabilities unavailable in any single existing product:
Role-specific question banks and company-pattern aptitude tests dynamically generated by AI, not static PDFs
A face-to-face AI mock interviewer that conducts dynamic, adaptive conversations in real-time using voice — not a quiz form
A post-session feedback engine that scores technical knowledge, communication quality, and confidence, then generates an actionable improvement report
This is a mini project built as a functional MVP for college placement training departments and individual students. The system is web-based, mobile-friendly, and built entirely on free-tier and affordable APIs during development, making it a zero-to-low-cost student project with real-world commercial viability.

2. Problem Statement
Every engineering college in India runs a placement training program. The current approach has three fundamental failures:
2.1  The Static Content Problem
Placement training departments hand students a PDF of 200 questions and say 'study this.' The problem: Amazon in 2025 does not ask the same questions they asked in 2019. TCS XE pattern is different from TCS NQT. A Data Analyst role at a startup is different from one at a MNC. Static question banks are outdated the moment they are printed.
2.2  The Missing Simulation Problem
Students can memorize answers but they have never sat in front of a real interviewer. They do not know what it feels like when an interviewer counter-questions their answer. They do not know how to maintain composure. They have no feedback mechanism on their actual spoken communication. Reading answers is NOT the same as delivering them live.
2.3  The Blind Feedback Problem
After a mock interview (if any is conducted), the feedback is subjective — a human trainer saying 'you were nervous' or 'your answer was okay.' There is no data. No score. No tracking improvement over time. Students do not know which specific topics they are weak in, which types of aptitude questions they consistently fail, or how their confidence level changes over sessions.

AI-Powered Features
Dynamic Question Generation: AI generates fresh questions for each session using company pattern parameters. No two sessions have identical questions.
Adaptive Difficulty: If the student gets 3 consecutive correct answers, difficulty increases. If 2 consecutive wrong, difficulty decreases. Final score adjusts accordingly.
Step-by-Step Solution: For every wrong answer, AI generates a detailed solution explaining the concept, the shortcut method, and a tip to remember it.

Weak Topic Radar: After each session, system identifies which sub-topics the student consistently fails and highlights them for focused practice.

5.3  Module 3: Face-to-Face AI Mock Interviewer
Purpose - The Core Feature
This is the feature that makes PrepAI genuinely different from every other platform in the market. Not a text-based Q&A. Not a form. A real-time voice conversation where an AI acts as an interviewer - asks questions, listens to the student's answers, processes those answers, and asks intelligent follow-up questions. This simulates the psychological pressure of a real interview, which is something no static platform can provide.
Interview Types Supported
HR Round: Behavioral questions, situational questions, salary/career goal discussions, company culture fit assessment
Technical Round: Role-specific technical concepts, problem-solving, knowledge depth testing
Combined Round: HR + Technical mixed, as many MNC first rounds are structured
Resume-Based Interview: Student uploads resume, AI extracts projects and skills, asks questions specifically about what is written on the resume
Case Study Interview: For PM, BA, and consulting roles - AI presents a business problem and discusses solutions

Conversation Flow Logic
The conversation engine follows a structured flow managed by the backend:
Phase 1 - Introduction (2-3 turns): AI greets, asks student to introduce themselves. Evaluates communication confidence from intro.
Phase 2 - Role Verification (2-3 turns): AI asks about why this role, what the student knows about the company. Sets the tone.
Phase 3 - Core Questions (8-12 turns): Main interview questions specific to role and company. AI uses previous answers to determine follow-ups.
Phase 4 - Situational / Behavioral (3-5 turns): STAR-format scenarios for non-technical rounds. Technical problem-solving for SDE rounds.
Phase 5 - Candidate Questions (1-2 turns): AI acts as interviewer and asks 'Do you have any questions for us?' - evaluates if student asks smart questions.
Phase 6 - Wrap-up: AI says session is complete, system processes transcript.
AI System Prompt Architecture
The AI interviewer operates with a carefully engineered system prompt that controls its behavior:
The system prompt contains: Role context (what role is being interviewed for), Company context (company style - formal like TCS vs. startup casual), Interview round type, Student's resume data (if uploaded), Conversation history (passed as messages array each turn), Evaluation criteria (what to probe deeper on), Response format rules (ask ONE question per turn, never reveal scoring, never break character).
Counter-Question Intelligence
This is what makes the AI feel like a human interviewer. When a student gives an answer, the AI does NOT move to the next scripted question. It analyzes the answer and chooses one of four responses:
Shallow answer detected: AI probes deeper - 'You mentioned X, can you explain how that works technically?'
Good answer given: AI challenges with a harder follow-up - 'You handled that well. Now what if the requirement changed to Y?'
Wrong/weak answer: AI gives a hint and rephrases - 'Let me put it another way. Have you worked with Z before?'
Complete answer: AI acknowledges and moves to next topic - 'That's a strong answer. Let's move to our next area.'

9. AI Prompt Engineering Design
The quality of PrepAI's AI features entirely depends on the system prompts used. Below are the production-grade prompts designed for each AI function.
9.1  Interview System Prompt (Master Template)
This prompt is sent as the system message in every interview API call:
INTERVIEW SYSTEM PROMPT
You are {PERSONA_NAME}, an experienced interviewer at {COMPANY_NAME} conducting a {INTERVIEW_TYPE} interview for the role of {JOB_ROLE}.

YOUR BEHAVIOR RULES:
- Ask exactly ONE question per response. Never ask multiple questions in one turn.
- When the candidate gives a shallow or incomplete answer, probe deeper with a follow-up question before moving on.
- When the candidate gives a strong answer, acknowledge it briefly (one sentence) and proceed to the next topic.
- Maintain your interviewer persona. Do not break character. Do not reveal scores or evaluation.
- Adapt your question difficulty based on the candidate's answer quality. If they struggle, simplify the next question. If they excel, increase depth.
- Keep responses concise. Your role is to ASK, not to teach.
- After 8-12 questions across all phases, signal the interview is wrapping up.

CANDIDATE CONTEXT:
Name: {CANDIDATE_NAME}
Resume Highlights: {RESUME_SUMMARY}
Target Company: {COMPANY_NAME}
Interview Type: {INTERVIEW_TYPE}

CURRENT PHASE: {CURRENT_PHASE}
PHASE INSTRUCTIONS: {PHASE_SPECIFIC_INSTRUCTIONS}

EVALUATION (internal - do not reveal):
After each candidate answer, silently assess: Technical Accuracy (1-5), Clarity (1-5), Depth (1-5), Relevance (1-5). Store in JSON format for post-interview processing.

Peer Practice Mode: Connect two students for practice interviews with each other - AI acts as observer and gives feedback to both
Phase 3 Features (6-12 months)
Custom Fine-Tuned Model: Fine-tune a smaller open-source model (Mistral / LLaMA) on 10,000+ real interview transcripts for dramatically better interview simulation
Company Integrations: Tie-up with companies to provide official practice materials - similar to how GRE has official test prep
Placement Predictor: ML model that takes student's PrepAI scores and predicts shortlist probability for specific companies
Live Coaching Sessions: Connect students with real placement coaches for paid 1:1 sessions, with AI handling scheduling and pre/post session analysis
Mobile App (React Native): Dedicated mobile app for on-the-go practice
Scalability Considerations
WebSocket handling: Use Redis Pub/Sub to scale WebSocket connections across multiple backend instances
AI API costs: Implement aggressive caching - cache question bank by role/company, never generate the same question twice
Audio processing: Move Whisper to a dedicated GPU instance as usage scales, or use Deepgram as a cheaper alternative
Database: Partition interview_sessions table by user_id for query performance at scale
CDN: Serve audio files from Cloudflare CDN to reduce latency for all India users
14. Conclusion
PrepAI addresses a genuine and large problem in India's engineering college ecosystem. Every year, approximately 1.5 million engineering students enter the placement market with inadequate preparation - not because resources do not exist, but because the resources that exist do not simulate the actual experience of being in an interview.
The three pillars of PrepAI - Role-Specific Preparation, Dynamic Aptitude Testing, and Face-to-Face AI Mock Interviews - together create the first complete placement preparation experience that a student can access from their laptop or phone, at zero cost during development and at a price point accessible to any college student commercially.
The technical implementation leverages modern AI APIs (Claude, Whisper, ElevenLabs) in a practical, cost-efficient architecture that students can build with existing college-level knowledge of React, Python, and API integration. The project is complex enough to be a genuine final year project but modular enough that each feature module stands independently.
From an innovation standpoint, the Face-to-Face AI Mock Interview module is entirely novel in the Indian college placement space. No product currently offers an AI that conducts a dynamic, voice-based, adaptive interview conversation for Indian students at this price point. This is the core intellectual contribution of PrepAI.
If this project were to be taken commercial post-college, the addressable market is 15+ million engineering students across India entering placement season annually. The 2026 campus placement landscape has structurally shifted — AI specialisation has split fresher offers from Rs. 3.5 LPA at the floor to over Rs. 1 crore at the very top, and mass IT hiring (TCS, Infosys, Wipro) is down 18% YoY, forcing students to compete harder and prepare smarter. PrepAI's AI-native approach — bilingual Tamil/English support, India company-pattern aptitude, and voice mock interviews at Rs. 32/student/month operational cost — gives it a structural advantage over US-focused tools like Final Round AI and Yoodli that have zero India customisation and charge $20-50/month in USD.
